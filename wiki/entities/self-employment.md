# Self-Employment (Career Field)

## Description
Self-employment grants Mediators independence and creative control, whether as freelancers, consultants, or business owners. It demands a solid plan and willingness to fail. Their human insight and charisma help build relationships, though aversion to sales pressure can be a hurdle. Success comes from ethical ideas and strong vendor/client connections.
