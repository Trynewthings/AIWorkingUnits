# Service Industry (Career Field)

## Description
Service industry roles offer Mediators the chance to make a meaningful difference through personalized customer interactions. Positions include food service, hospitality, retail, and tour guiding. A good cultural fit is crucial; high-end businesses may offer strong compensation. These jobs can provide flexibility for side pursuits or entrepreneurial growth.
