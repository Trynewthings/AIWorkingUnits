# Carol Dweck

## Description
Carol Dweck is a renowned professor of psychology known for her theory of “fixed” vs. “growth” mindsets, which describes how individuals’ beliefs about their abilities affect motivation and learning. Her work is especially relevant for Mediators, who may struggle with balancing innate talents and the need for disciplined effort.

## Appearances
- [Mediator Guide to Careers](../sources/mediator-guide-to-careers.md): introduced as a key figure in understanding Mediator learning challenges.
- [Facts Thrive with Context to Appealing Only to Instinct](../sources/facts-thrive-to-appealing-to-instinct.md): provides detailed steps for Mediators to adopt a growth mindset by reframing failure as an opportunity and gathering feedback.
