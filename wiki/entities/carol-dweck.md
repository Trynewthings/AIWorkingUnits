# Carol Dweck

## Description
Carol Dweck is a renowned professor of psychology known for developing the **Growth Mindset** theory — a framework distinguishing between fixed and growth orientations toward intelligence and ability. Her work focuses on motivation, learning, and the consequences of believing that abilities are either innate/fixed or developable through effort.

In the context of the *Mediator Guide to Careers*, Dweck's framework is presented as a key corrective tool for Mediators who are prone to fixed mindset thinking — particularly those who were identified as gifted early in life and struggle when natural talent alone is no longer sufficient.
