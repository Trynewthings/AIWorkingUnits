# Explorer Personality Type

## Description

In the 16Personalities framework, Explorers are personality types that share Sensing (S) and Perceiving (P) traits: ESTP (Entrepreneur) and ESFP (Entertainer). They are practical, hands-on, action-oriented, and thrive on solving immediate, concrete problems. Explorers are often spontaneous, adaptable, and highly creative when engaged with a tangible challenge. They tend to be less interested in abstract philosophy or lengthy theoretical discussions, preferring tasks that yield visible results and a sense of discovery.

## Relationships

Mediators (INFP) and Explorers can combine complementary strengths. Mediators bring deep empathy, big-picture creativity, and human insight, while Explorers contribute decisiveness, hands-on problem-solving, and the ability to take immediate action. For effective collaboration:

- Mediators should frame requests as clear, concrete problems (“Find the bug on this page” rather than “Improve our image”).
- Avoid long-winded explanations; engage Explorers with urgency and fun.
- Learn from Explorers’ behavior in high-pressure situations and experiment with their bias toward action.

Mutual appreciation for each other’s distinct approaches can cover weaknesses and unite both types in pursuit of innovation.
