# Education (Career Field)

## Description
Teaching and educational roles suit Mediators' imagination, empathy, and desire to see students as individuals. From preschool to adult education, these careers allow them to inspire and connect beyond conventional metrics. Other roles include guidance counselor, school psychologist, sports coach, and religious educator.
