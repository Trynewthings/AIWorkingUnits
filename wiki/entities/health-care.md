# Health Care (Career Field)

## Description
Health and wellness careers allow Mediators to exercise empathy and creativity while helping others achieve self-discovery and improved well-being. Roles range from medicine and nursing to counseling and alternative health. Ideal positions offer one-on-one patient interaction and time for reflection.
