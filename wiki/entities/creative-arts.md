# Creative Arts (Career Field)

## Description
The Creative Arts is identified as one of the primary suitable career fields for Mediators in the *Mediator Guide to Careers*. It encompasses a broad range of roles including:

- **Writing**: Freelance writing, staff writer at magazines/publications, content creation
- **Visual/Commercial Art**: Brand-strategy firms, commercial art, illustration
- **Architecture**: Requires formal credentials/degrees; allows grand creative execution
- **Digital/Interactive**: Web design, video game design, multimedia production (formal programming training helpful)
- **Self-publishing and independent creation**: Fiction, film, streaming content — technology enables reaching audiences without full-time arts employment

The guide notes that Mediators in creative careers need not be "starving artists" — sustainable income is achievable through freelance work, corporate creative roles, or side projects that complement a primary income.
