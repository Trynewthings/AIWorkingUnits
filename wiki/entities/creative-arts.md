# Creative Arts (Career Field)

## Description
Creative arts encompass fine and commercial artistic roles, including artist, musician, crafts, architecture, and writing. Mediators can find sustainable careers in these fields as freelancers or within organizations, often leveraging formal training to open doors. When financial pressure is high, they can pursue artistic passions as side projects through self-publishing and online platforms.
