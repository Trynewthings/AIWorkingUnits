# Public Service and Social Work (Career Field)

## Description
Public service and social work appeal to Mediators' ethical standards and caring nature. Despite sensitivity, they can handle tragic situations by focusing on direct service. Options include social work, nonprofit roles, librarianship, community organizing, and environmental positions (conservation, park services, etc.).
