# Part: Professional Development

## Setting
The second major section of the *Mediator Guide to Careers*, covering career selection, workplace roles, and inter-type cooperation strategies for Mediators.

## Events
- **Suitable Careers for Mediators** — Overview of career fields that suit Mediators; Creative Arts introduced as a primary category. Key insight: ask "Who do I want to be?" not "What do I want to be?"
- **Career Alternatives** — (To be expanded on further ingestion)
- **Workplace Roles** — Three sub-roles examined:
  - Mediators as Subordinates
  - Mediators as Colleagues
  - Mediators as Managers
- **Workplace Cooperation Between Types** — Four cooperation pairings:
  - Mediator–Analyst
  - Mediator–Diplomat
  - Mediator–Sentinel
  - Mediator–Explorer
- **Conclusion** — (To be expanded)

## Notable passages
> "Rare, complex, and enigmatic, Mediators seek careers that are more than just jobs. Whatever their profession, they need a role that enables them to support worthy causes and exercise creative freedom."

> "The most important question for Mediators to ask when searching for a career isn't 'What do I want to be?' but rather 'Who do I want to be?'"

> "With inspiration, even the most data-driven or technical field can be pursued as an art, whether coding a video game or riveting an airplane."
