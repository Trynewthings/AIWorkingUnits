# Hypersensitivity to Criticism

## Definition
A common challenge for Mediators, who may take critical feedback as a personal rebuke rather than an impersonal evaluation, leading to hurt feelings, withdrawal, or uncharacteristic outbursts of anger.

## Evidence
- *Source: [Hypersensitive to Criticism -> Facing Conflict](../sources/hypersensitive-criticism-to-facing-conflict.md)* covers the emotional impact of criticism on Mediators and the need to develop resilience.
- In *[Caring or Naïve? — Second Opinions](../sources/caring-or-naive-to-second-opinions.md)*, the “Nothing Personal” section offers concrete strategies to reduce sensitivity:
  - Identify personal beliefs about how others “should” communicate feedback.
  - Examine the interpretations that make feedback feel like a personal attack.
  - In triggering moments, generate alternative explanations (e.g., “Could this be about efficiency, not a personal slight?”).
  The guide emphasizes that Analysts’ bluntness is rarely personal, helping Mediators to lower their defenses.

## Open questions
- How can Mediators distinguish between truly toxic criticism and feedback that merely feels hurtful due to their sensitivity?
- What protective practices can Mediators use in the moment to pause before reacting?
