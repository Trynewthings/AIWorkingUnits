# Infectious Positivity

## Definition
Infectious Positivity describes the phenomenon where Mediators who feel appreciated and validated at work become exceptionally loyal, enthusiastic, and proactive. Their positive energy spreads to colleagues, improving overall team morale and productivity. This dynamic is contingent on a supportive environment with gentle correction and genuine recognition.

## Evidence
- Source: [Creative Arts to Helpful Accountability](../sources/creative-arts-to-helpful-accountability.md) — "Managers who recognize excellence and correct errors gently can expect strong commitment and cheerfulness from Mediator employees. … Their positive energy is infectious, brightening the workday for their peers."

## Open questions
- How can Mediators cultivate positivity in unsupportive or critical workplaces?
- Is there a risk of burnout if the need for appreciation becomes excessive?
