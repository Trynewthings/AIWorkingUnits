# Solidifying the Abstract

## Definition
The Mediator's struggle to translate broad, interconnected ideas into concrete, sequential actions. This involves bridging the gap between their Intuitive vision and practical implementation.

## Evidence
- Mediators' minds can skip from Step A to Z, leaving them with no starting point. [Source: Tempered Idealism to Restless Hearts](../sources/tempered-idealism-to-restless-hearts.md)
- A creative planning exercise: Visualize true happiness, set a weekly goal, list 10 steps to achieve it. This creates a well-researched timeline. [ibid]

## Strategies
- Morning/evening reflection: perform the planning exercise daily and observe changes.
- Start small: tiny changes accumulate into significant progress.
- Use research to fill in missing steps (see [Research and Idealism](../sources/tempered-idealism-to-restless-hearts.md#research-and-idealism)).

## Open questions
- How can Mediators maintain motivation when the steps feel mundane compared to the grand vision?
