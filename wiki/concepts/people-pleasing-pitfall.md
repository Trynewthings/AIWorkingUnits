# People-Pleasing Pitfall

## Definition
Mediators' desire to maintain harmony and avoid disappointing others can lead to people-pleasing, where they prioritize others' needs over their own, fail to set boundaries, and accept subpar behavior to keep the peace.

## Evidence
- [Source: Creating Alone Time to Hiding Hurt Feelings](../sources/creating-alone-time-to-hiding-hurt-feelings.md): As managers, Mediators may let bad behavior slide because they are averse to criticizing others and always see the best in people, ultimately harming team morale.
- [Source: Tempered Idealism to Restless Hearts](../sources/tempered-idealism-to-restless-hearts.md)

- What are the long-term consequences of people-pleasing on team dynamics?
