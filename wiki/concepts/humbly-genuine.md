# Humbly Genuine

## Definition
The "Humbly Genuine" approach is a strategy for Mediators (INFPs) to navigate academic or professional situations where they must engage with ideas they disagree with, without compromising authenticity or creating unnecessary conflict. Inspired by improvisational theater's "Yes, and..." rule, it involves accepting a given premise and creatively adding one's own perspective, thus maintaining harmony while staying true to one's values.

## Evidence
- **Mediator College Advice** describes the technique in the context of college courses: Mediators can treat the classroom as an improv club, accepting the standard thesis of a lecture as the recognized premise and then adding their own flourishes. This helps them enjoy the feeling of authenticity while avoiding clashes. (sources/mediator-college-advice.md)
