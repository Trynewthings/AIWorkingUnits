# Helpful Accountability

## Definition
Helpful Accountability refers to strategies Mediators can use to combat perfectionism and procrastination through external structure. Despite preferring independence, they benefit from regular check-ins with managers or colleagues, strict deadlines, and accepting "good enough" work. This approach helps them move past passive idealism and deliver timely results.

## Evidence
- Source: [Creative Arts to Helpful Accountability](../sources/creative-arts-to-helpful-accountability.md) — "Counter to their own intuitions, Mediators often find that strict deadlines help them … break their habit of passive idealism and perfectionism."
