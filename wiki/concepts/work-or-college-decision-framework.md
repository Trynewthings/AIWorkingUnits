# Work or College Decision Framework

## Definition
A structured approach to help Mediators decide whether to commit to a new academic or career path, overcoming their tendency to act on impulse or overthink.

## Framework from Tempered Idealism
- **Research before committing**: evaluate options using a checklist:
  - Location and schedule: Can they make it physically/logistically?
  - Instructor: Are they well-regarded? Check reviews and ask peers.
  - Cost: Can they afford it? Are there cheaper community alternatives?
  - Overcommitment: Do they have the time and energy to fully engage?
  - Outside opinions: Check internet reviews, ask friends, contact the instructor during office hours.
- This exhaustive approach prevents long-term mismatches.
