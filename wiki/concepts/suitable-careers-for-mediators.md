# Suitable Careers for Mediators

## Definition
The guide frames career suitability for Mediators around purpose, creative freedom, and human connection rather than salary, title, or prestige. The central question is not "What do I want to be?" but "Who do I want to be?" — meaning career choice is an expression of identity and values, not just a means to income.

**General criteria for a suitable Mediator career:**
- Enables support of worthy causes
- Allows creative freedom
- Involves meaningful human connection (not purely data/analysis-driven)
- Provides enough autonomy to avoid prolonged, draining social contact
- Avoids routine, strictly impersonal work, and analysis of minor details as primary job functions

**Career fields introduced so far:**
- **Creative Arts**: Writing (staff writer, freelancer), architecture, web/game design, multimedia production, commercial art. Note: formal credentials (degrees, training) can open doors even in creative fields. Side projects and self-publishing are viable options for balancing financial demands with creative impulses.
