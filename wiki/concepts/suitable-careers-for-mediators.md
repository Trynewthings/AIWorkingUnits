# Suitable Careers for Mediators

## Definition
A curated list of career fields and roles that align with the INFP / Mediator personality type, emphasizing empathy, creativity, independence, and meaning. The list evolves as new sources are ingested.

## Evidence
- [Source: Creative Arts to Helpful Accountability](../sources/creative-arts-to-helpful-accountability.md) adds: Creative Arts, Health Care, Education, Public Service and Social Work, Service Industry, Self-Employment, Professional Volunteerism.
- Previous sources also mention Creative Arts and other fields (see [Tempered Idealism to Restless Hearts](../sources/tempered-idealism-to-restless-hearts.md), [Solidifying the Abstract](../sources/solidifying-the-abstract.md)).

- What specific sub-roles best suit the Mediator's need for autonomy within each field?
