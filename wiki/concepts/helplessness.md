# Helplessness

## Definition
When overwhelmed by stress, unrealistic expectations, or repeated failure, Mediators may fall into learned helplessness, believing they are incapable of effecting change.

## Evidence
- [Source: Creating Alone Time to Hiding Hurt Feelings](../sources/creating-alone-time-to-hiding-hurt-feelings.md): Mediator managers facing crises and impossible deadlines may feel helpless and overwhelmed, leading to procrastination and distraction. However, setting realistic goals and celebrating progress can break the cycle.
- [Source: Facts Thrive with Context to Appealing Only to Instinct](../sources/facts-thrive-to-appealing-to-instinct.md)
