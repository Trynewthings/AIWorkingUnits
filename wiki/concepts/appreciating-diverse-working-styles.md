# Appreciating Diverse Working Styles

## Definition

Appreciating diverse working styles means recognizing that different personality types bring complementary strengths, and actively cultivating curiosity and respect for approaches that differ from one’s own—such as an Explorer’s decisiveness and hands-on problem-solving. For Mediators, this involves observing, learning, and even selectively adopting behaviors (e.g., taking immediate action) to enhance their own professional growth.

## Evidence

- **Source: [Calls to Action – So… What’s Next?](../sources/calls-to-action-to-whats-next.md)**: Mediators are encouraged to approach Explorer colleagues with “genuine curiosity,” note how they handle urgent situations, and experiment with replicating their behavior. The book suggests that Mediators can “marvel at the opportunity to work with people who are wholly different from themselves,” seeing even criticisms as a window into a different perspective.

## Open questions

- Where is the line between healthy adaptation and inauthentic mimicry of a different working style?
- Can this appreciation be systematically taught to Mediators who struggle with conflict or criticism from more pragmatic colleagues?
