# Need for Solitude

## Definition
Mediators require regular periods of solitude to recharge their energy, process emotions, and maintain their sense of self. Without alone time, they risk burnout and diminished well-being.

## Evidence
- [Source: Creating Alone Time to Hiding Hurt Feelings](../sources/creating-alone-time-to-hiding-hurt-feelings.md): Mediators burn out without pockets of alone time; creative strategies like headphones, solitary lunches, and negotiating independent tasks can carve out necessary solo time in open-plan offices. Monitoring personal tolerance levels helps anticipate when a break is needed.
- [Source: Solidifying the Abstract](../sources/solidifying-the-abstract.md)
