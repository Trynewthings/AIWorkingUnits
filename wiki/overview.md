# Overview: Mediator Guide to Careers

## Thesis
The *Mediator Guide to Careers* serves as a comprehensive career manual for individuals with the INFP (Mediator) personality type. It argues that Mediators thrive when they align their work with deeply held values, leverage their creativity and empathy, and proactively manage their characteristic weaknesses like sensitivity, conflict avoidance, and difficulty with practical execution.

## Plot/Argument arc
1. Introduction to the Mediator personality and its essential traits.
2. Exploration of career fields well-suited to Mediators (creative arts, education, helping professions, etc.).
3. **Professional development**: Strategies to overcome common workplace challenges – handling criticism, communicating with different types, staying organized, and seeking balance.
   - [Caring or Naïve? — Second Opinions](sources/caring-or-naive-to-second-opinions.md) provides concrete advice for communicating with Analysts (NT) and Diplomats (NF), and for actively seeking second opinions to ground idealism.
4. **Calls to action and conclusion**: framing requests for Explorer-type colleagues, cultivating appreciation for different working styles, and harmonizing work with personality as the path to job satisfaction. (See [Calls to Action – So… What's Next?](sources/calls-to-action-to-whats-next.md).)
