# Source: Calls to Action – So… What’s Next?

## Summary

The final chapter of the Mediator Guide to Careers offers practical advice for Mediators (INFPs) on working with Explorer personality types (ESTP/ESFP). It emphasizes the importance of framing requests concretely, avoiding long-winded philosophical explanations, and learning from Explorers’ action-oriented, curious approach. The conclusion reinforces the book’s central message: Mediators achieve job satisfaction by harmonizing their work with their personality—unhooking from praise and criticism, balancing alone time with social interaction, and embracing their creativity and empathy. The book ends with encouragement to pursue work aligned with personal values and to continue growing through the author’s other resources.

## Key claims

- Mediators can collaborate productively with Explorers by translating big ideas into concrete, present-moment problems that capture Explorers’ interest.
- Exploring immediate action, using colloquial language, and ending conversations with clear calls to action increase cooperation.
- Mediators should cultivate genuine curiosity about Explorers’ differing perspective and note the benefits of their decisiveness and ability to seize opportunities.
- Job satisfaction requires balancing sensitivity and optimism with the need to face conflict, manage criticism, and seek alternative viewpoints.
- Mediators’ ultimate motivation is creativity, curiosity, and a desire to be a force for good, not competition.
