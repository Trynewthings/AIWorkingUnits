# Sample Chapter: The Locked Door

Eleanor stood at the threshold of the manor's east wing, the brass key cold
between her fingers. Her grandfather had warned her — *never the east wing
after sunset* — but the letter she had found in the attic that morning
mentioned a name she had never heard: **Cassian Vale**.

The key turned with a reluctant click. Beyond the door lay a corridor whose
walls were lined with portraits, every one of them facing away.

> "Memory is not a record," her grandfather had once told her at the dinner
> table. "It is a negotiation."

Eleanor walked past the portraits until she came to a small study. On the
desk lay a journal bound in faded red leather, dated 1923. The first entry
began: *Cassian arrived today, and the household is changed.*

The themes of inheritance, of secrets withheld between generations, would
return to her again and again in the months that followed.
